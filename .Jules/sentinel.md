## 2026-03-30 - Sandbox Preview PostMessage Origin Validation Bypass
**Vulnerability:** In `resolveParentEmbedderOrigin`, the check `isSandboxPreviewGuestHost(guestHostname)` checked whether the guest (child window) was running on a `*.grok-sandbox.com` domain rather than checking if the candidate parent origin (`url.hostname`) was a trusted sandbox domain. When the app ran in a sandbox preview, `resolveParentEmbedderOrigin` returned any parent URL's origin (including `https://attacker.com`), allowing untrusted sites to frame the application and receive sensitive postMessage events (e.g. location updates, route paths).
**Learning:** Checking the identity of the local frame (`guestHostname`) instead of validating the remote frame (`url.hostname`) effectively bypasses origin restrictions when the app is hosted in a sandbox environment.
**Prevention:** Always validate the candidate origin (`url.hostname`) against the allowlist of trusted origins before establishing postMessage handlers or sending messages to `window.parent`.
